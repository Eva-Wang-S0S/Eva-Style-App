🌊 Mask
🌊 Sheet Mask Compress
1–2× per week
  • Cleanse and tone as usual
  • Soak cotton pad with Bioderma, wring till barely damp
  • Apply to full face 10–15 minutes
  • Remove and continue with eye cream + serum
  • This replaces the toner step
Best for Perth winter dryness. Use after any day skin feels tight. Don't combine with exfoliation same day.
🍯 Swisse Clay Mask
1× per week
  • Apply to dry, cleansed skin (avoid eye area)
  • Leave 10–15 min — remove before fully dry
  • Rinse thoroughly with lukewarm water
  • Follow with full hydration routine immediately
Manuka honey cleanses and hydrates simultaneously. In winter, follow-up moisturiser is essential. Avoid applying over active inflamed spots.
🌸 Exfoliation
1× per week · clear skin only
  • After cleansing, apply exfoliant as directed
  • Rinse and follow with full routine
  • Skip azelaic acid that same evening
  • Extra SPF the next morning
⚠️ Skip entirely when breaking out. In winter, max once a week to protect the skin barrier.
Rules
One treatment per day
Never layer sheet mask + clay mask + exfoliation on the same day.
Day after exfoliating
Skip azelaic acid if skin feels sensitive. Prioritise moisture and SPF.
