🌊 Hydrating Sheet Mask
1–2× per week · evenings
  • Cleanse and tone as usual
  • Soak cotton pad with Bioderma, wring out until barely damp
  • Apply to full face for 10–15 minutes
  • Remove and continue with eye cream + serum
  • Skip the toner step — this replaces it
Best treatment for Perth winter dryness. Do this after any day your skin feels tight or flaky. Cannot be combined with exfoliation on the same day.
🍯 Swisse Clay Mask
1× per week · evenings
  • Apply to dry, cleansed skin (avoid eye area)
  • Leave on 10–15 min — remove before fully dry
  • Rinse thoroughly with lukewarm water
  • Follow immediately with toner + serum + moisturiser
Manuka honey cleanses and hydrates at the same time. In winter, the follow-up moisturiser step is essential — don't skip it. If you're breaking out, avoid applying over active inflamed spots.
🌸 Exfoliation
1× per week · clear skin only
  • After cleansing, apply exfoliant as directed
  • Rinse and follow with full routine
  • Skip azelaic acid that same evening
  • Be extra thorough with SPF the next morning
⚠️ Skip entirely when breaking out — exfoliating over inflamed skin makes things worse. In winter, limit to once a week max to protect the skin barrier.
Scheduling Rules
One treatment per day
Never layer sheet mask + clay mask + exfoliation on the same day. Pick one and save the rest for other days.
Day-after exfoliation
The evening after exfoliating, you can skip azelaic acid if skin feels sensitive. Prioritise moisture and SPF.
